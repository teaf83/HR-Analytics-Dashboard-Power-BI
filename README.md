# HR-Analytics-Dashboard-Power-BI

Power BI dashboard built on 100,000 employee records across 9 departments and 7 job titles

KPIs
Total Employees: 100K
Average Salary: $6.40K
Attrition Rate: 10.01%
Attrition Count: 10.01K
Average Performance Score: 3.00 / 5
Employee Satisfaction: 3.00 / 5

Visuals
KPI cards — top-level totals with icons
Average Salary by Performance (bar) — salary rises clearly with performance score (1→5)
Average Salary by Job Title (funnel bar) — Engineer/Manager highest ($7.80K), Technician lowest ($4.55K)
Average Salary by Department (bar) — nearly flat across all 9 departments (~$6.4K each)
Count of Employee by Job Title (donut) — roughly even split, ~14% per title
Count of Employee by Gender (donut) — near 50/50 split
Filters — Department, Job_Title, Education_Level, Gender
Key Insight

Salary correlates strongly with performance score and job title, but shows almost no variation across departments. Attrition (10%) is also nearly identical across all departments — it does not point to a specific problem area.

Tool
Power BI Desktop.
